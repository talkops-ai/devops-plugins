Invoke **instrumenting-kubernetes-workloads**, **provisioning-opentelemetry-collectors**, **onboarding-exporters-and-instrumentation**, **configuring-prometheus-scraping-and-discovery**, and **authoring-and-testing-prometheus-rules** (plus **authoring-loki-alerting-and-recording-rules** when log alerts are needed and **mapping-tempo-topology-and-alerts** when trace topology/span-metrics alerts are needed) to set up production-grade Kubernetes monitoring:
1. Inspect the workload runtime, exposed ports/endpoints, existing OpenTelemetry collectors, and Prometheus Operator CRDs in the cluster.
2. Configure zero-code OpenTelemetry auto-instrumentation or appropriate exporter sidecars/deployments with cardinality-safe OTTL filtering (**controlling-telemetry-cardinality-and-noise**).
3. Author and validate `ServiceMonitor` / `PodMonitor` / `Probe` resources and verify active target discovery in Prometheus.
4. Author and unit-test recording rules and multi-window, multi-burn-rate SLO `PrometheusRule` alerts wired cleanly to Alertmanager routing (**validating-alertmanager-routing**). When `--signals` scopes to `alerting` (or `metrics,alerting`), focus directly on SLO `PrometheusRule` authoring and live Alertmanager route validation without unnecessary OTel collector changes. When `--signals` scopes to `logs`, focus directly on inspecting Loki schema/Ruler groups and authoring, backtesting, validating, and dry-run previewing Loki log SLO error-ratio and `absent_over_time` heartbeat rules (**authoring-loki-alerting-and-recording-rules**) without unnecessary OTel collector or `PrometheusRule` changes. When `--signals` scopes to `traces` (or OpenTelemetry collector/SDK onboarding), focus on surveying and provisioning OpenTelemetry Collector pipelines (**provisioning-opentelemetry-collectors**), configuring protocol-aligned `Instrumentation` CRDs or zero-code eBPF `OBI` (**instrumenting-kubernetes-workloads**), and wiring cardinality-safe OTTL filters (**controlling-telemetry-cardinality-and-noise**) and pre-sampling `spanmetrics`/`servicegraph` connectors (**tuning-sampling-and-trace-derived-metrics**). When `--signals` scopes to `traces`, focus directly on inspecting Grafana Tempo capabilities (`tempo://server/capabilities`, `tempo://deployment/overview`), mapping upstream/downstream service dependencies (`tempo_get_service_dependencies`), measuring observed RED error and latency baselines (`tempo_analyze_service_red`), and generating Tempo `span-metrics` and `service-graphs` SLO `PrometheusRule` manifests (**mapping-tempo-topology-and-alerts**) calibrated to the requested `--slo` target.
